Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Introduction

Mikhail Gudzikevich ("we", "our", "us") operates Localhosts, a menu bar app for Mac that starts, stops and opens the local websites you run on your computer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles.

In short: everything stays on your Mac. Localhosts does not send anything to us or to anyone else, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and does not connect to the internet.

How the App Works

  • For each site you add, Localhosts runs the command you entered in the folder you chose, and shows the output of that command in its log window.
  • To show whether a site is running, the app checks on your Mac which local ports are in use and which processes listen on them (process ID, name and working folder). This information is read on your Mac and is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • When you open a site, the app asks your default browser to open its local address, such as http://name.localhost:8080. When you show a site's folder, it opens Finder.
  • The app itself does not make network requests. The commands you run are your own programs: what they do, including any network access, is up to them and is not covered by this policy.

Information Stored on Your Mac

  • The list of your sites: name, folder, command, port, address, whether the site starts with the app, when it was added, and the process ID of its last launch.
  • Your setting for what happens to running sites when you quit the app.
  • If you turn on opening at login, macOS keeps this in its own list of login items, which you can change in System Settings.

This information stays on your Mac, in the app's storage. We do not upload it and the app does not sync it to any cloud service. If backups are enabled, it may be included in your Time Machine or other backup, which is controlled by you. The log of command output is kept only in memory (the last 500 lines per site) and is cleared when you quit the app.

Purchase and Download

  • Localhosts is sold outside the Mac App Store, through BundleHunt. Your payment and the details you give at checkout are processed by BundleHunt and its payment providers under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details.
  • The app is downloaded from GitHub, and these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub receives standard technical information when you open a page or download a file, such as your IP address and browser type, under GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • We receive information only if you choose to email us. We use your email only to answer you.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require accounts, logins or email addresses.
  • We do not operate servers that receive data from the app.
  • We do not collect your sites, commands, folders, logs or the contents of your projects.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting services, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or share personal information. BundleHunt processes purchases and GitHub hosts our downloads and pages, as described above.

Data Retention

  • Your sites and settings stay on your Mac until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its data.
  • If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as needed to help you, and delete it on request.

Your Rights

You control the data in the app: you can edit or delete any site and change the settings at any time. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to access, correct or delete that correspondence.

Children's Privacy

The app is a tool for developers and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
