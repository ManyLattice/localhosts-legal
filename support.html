Localhosts Support

Localhosts lives in the Mac menu bar and starts, stops and opens the local websites you run on your computer.

Need help or found a bug? Email hakarim81@gmail.com. Please include the app version, your macOS version, the site's command and port, and the last lines of its log.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I download the app?

From the download link, which always gives the latest version. Open the DMG and drag Localhosts to Applications. The app requires macOS 26 or later.

I can't find the app after launching it.

Localhosts has no Dock icon. Look for its icon in the menu bar at the top right of the screen.

A site says its port is busy.

Another program already listens on that port. Stop that program or choose another port for the site. If the program serves the site from its folder but was started elsewhere, Localhosts shows it as started elsewhere, and turning the site off stops that program.

A site stops right after it starts.

Open its log: it shows the output of the command and its exit code. Check that the command works in Terminal from the same folder.

How do I open the app at login?

Turn on opening at login in the app's settings. You can also choose whether running sites stop or keep running when you quit the app.

How do I get a refund?

Localhosts is sold through BundleHunt. Refunds follow BundleHunt's policy: contact BundleHunt support with your order details.

Does Localhosts send my data anywhere?

No. Your sites, commands and logs stay on your Mac, and the app does not connect to the internet. See the Privacy Policy.
